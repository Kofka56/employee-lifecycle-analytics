No strong standalone predictors of offer status were identified among the tested candidate characteristics.

Offer rates by education are tightly clustered at 19.1%–22.3%.

Within the 3–5 years experience group, offered share ranges from 8.7% for 30–45k to 27.8% for 90k+.
